What
{in}: the file · {out}: the folder for what it makes (what's in it comes back) · {name}: the file's name without its ending · {file}: with it. It runs on whichever device takes the file, as your account.
On
{in}, {out}, {name}, {file}: the file, the folder for what it makes (what's in it comes back), its name without and with its ending · {input}: the line or number · {i}, {n}: the part's place and how many there are · {member}: the member running it. A script gets the file, line or number as $1, and all of them as IN, OUT, NAME, INPUT, INDEX, COUNT and MEMBER; it runs by its #! line, else by its ending (bash, python3, node, perl). Each device runs them (this PC in its own Linux), as its own user; as root only with the cluster password, which each member checks itself.

Add a device to the cluster

  1. Raspberry Pi OS (64-bit) on the device, with Tailscale installed and logged in to your account.
  2. On the device, from your own account (not root), run this. It asks for your password once: curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alexandermulholland92/hand-tracker/main/cluster/setup.sh | sudo bash Copied.
  3. It shows up here within a minute. From then on it keeps itself up to date (each part has a switch under ⚙).
