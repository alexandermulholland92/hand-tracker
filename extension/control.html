Hand mouse
Turn it on, then go to any other tab: a pointer follows your palm over that page. A quick curl of your index finger clicks (curl and hold it to drag), a quick curl of your middle finger right-clicks, and curling both (or a fist) holds the pointer still while you move your hand back to the middle. Keep this tab open: it's pinned at the left of your tabs. Alt+Shift+M turns it on and off from anywhere in Chrome.
Hand mouse off
Gesture actions
A gesture can press keys, type, click or scroll in the page in front. Each one has its own switch.
What it can't do
It works web pages only: not Chrome's own controls (tabs, the address bar), its own pages (a new tab, settings), the Web Store, or other apps. For the whole computer, use the Hand Tracker app. A few sites ignore clicks that don't come from a real mouse.
