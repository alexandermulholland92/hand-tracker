Pi Cluster

Looking for Pis over Tailscale…

Add a Pi to the cluster

  1. Raspberry Pi OS (64-bit) on the Pi, with Tailscale installed and logged in to your account.
  2. On the Pi, from your own account (not root), run this. It asks for your password once: curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alexandermulholland92/hand-tracker/main/cluster/setup.sh | sudo bash Copied.
  3. It shows up here within a minute. From then on it keeps itself up to date (each part has a switch under ⚙).

Taking a Pi out: unplug it and press Forget here, or remove the service from it with curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alexandermulholland92/hand-tracker/main/cluster/setup.sh | sudo bash -s -- --remove
