Hand browser
Open a website and it fills the screen, the camera in a small window in a corner, and your hand moves a pointer over it. A quick curl of your index finger taps, a quick flick of your open hand up or down swipes the page (curl and move to drag it), a quick curl of your middle finger right-clicks; gesture actions can scroll, press keys and type. The small window's ⚙ brings Hand Mouse back full screen.
