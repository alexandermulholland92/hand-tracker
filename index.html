        YawTurning the hand left or right — like shaking your head "no." Shown in the arrow's direction on each card.
        PitchTilting the palm up or down — like nodding your head "yes," or tipping a tray forward/back.
        RollRotating the hand like turning a doorknob — this is what flips between "palm facing you" and "back of hand facing you."

        Control your PC
        Control this phone
        Your hand moves a pointer over any app, like the hand mouse on a PC: a quick curl of your index finger taps, curl it and move your hand to swipe or drag (the swipe follows your hand), a quick curl of your middle finger is a long press. Gesture actions can press Back, Home, Recents, volume and media keys, or type. It keeps going with Hand Tracker in the background: a small camera window stays in a corner (its × stops it).
        "Restricted setting"? Android 13 and later block this for apps installed from a downloaded file. Tap Open App info, then ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings, and confirm with your PIN or fingerprint. Then tap 2. Turn on hand control in Accessibility again. (⋮ offers it only once the "Restricted setting" box has appeared; installing a newer Hand Tracker keeps it allowed.)
        Hand browser
        iPhones and iPads don't let an app tap inside other apps, so here the hand mouse works websites: open one and it fills the screen, the camera in a small window in a corner, and your hand moves a pointer over it. A quick curl of your index finger taps, curl it and move your hand to swipe the page, a quick curl of your middle finger right-clicks; gesture actions can scroll, press keys and type. The small window's ⚙ brings Hand Tracker back full screen. To work every app on this iPhone, a computer with Hand Tracker can be its Bluetooth mouse (see the README).

            Luxonis OAK camera

            With an OAK camera (OAK-D, OAK-D Lite, OAK-1…) the hands are found on the camera itself, and an OAK-D also measures each hand's distance. It needs a one-time setup: Hand Tracker downloads its own copy of Python 3.12 with Luxonis's depthai library, OpenCV and NumPy, and the camera models (about 150 MB, into its own folder; nothing else on your computer changes). The tracking code comes from geaxgx/depthai_hand_tracker.
