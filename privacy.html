Hand Mouse Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · for the Hand Mouse app for Android (com.handtracker.mouse)

In short: Hand Mouse works entirely on your phone. It doesn't collect, store on a server, sell or share any of your data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

The camera

Hand Mouse uses your camera to find your hand and follow it, so you can move a pointer and tap without touching the screen. The pictures are processed on your phone, as they come in, by a hand-tracking model built into the app. They are not saved, and they are never sent anywhere.

Controlling this phone (Accessibility)

If you turn on Hand Mouse's hand control in Settings → Accessibility, Hand Mouse uses Android's Accessibility service to act for you while you use your hand instead of the screen:

  • it taps, long-presses and swipes where the pointer is, and presses Back, Home, Recents, volume and media keys;
  • it reads the text box you're typing in, and only that, so it can type into it. It doesn't read anything else on the screen;
  • it only acts while you've started "This phone" in Hand Mouse.

Nothing it does or reads leaves your phone. The app asks for your agreement before it opens the Accessibility settings, and you can turn hand control off at any time there.

Display over other apps

While you control your phone, Hand Mouse draws its pointer and a small camera window over the apps you use. This shows only on your screen.

Controlling a computer

You can use Hand Mouse as the mouse and keyboard of your own computer, either over Bluetooth or over your local Wi-Fi network:

  • Bluetooth: your phone pairs with your computer as a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard and sends it pointer movements, clicks and keys.
  • Wi-Fi: you pair by scanning a code your computer shows. Your phone then sends pointer movements, clicks, keys and text straight to that computer on your network, signed with a key only the two of them know.

These go only to the computer you paired with, never to us or anyone else.

Browsing with your hand

Hand Mouse can open websites for you to browse by hand. As in any browser, the websites you open receive your requests and may use their own cookies under their own privacy policies. Hand Mouse doesn't send the websites you visit anywhere.

Permissions

  • Camera: to follow your hand.
  • Display over other apps: for the pointer and the camera window.
  • Accessibility (hand control): to tap, swipe and type for you, as described above.
  • Foreground service (camera) and notifications: to keep following your hand while you use other apps, with a notification showing it's on.
  • Bluetooth: to act as your computer's mouse and keyboard.
  • Network: to reach your computer over Wi-Fi, and websites you open.

Children

Hand Mouse isn't directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.
