Remote recording

Start and stop recording on a computer running Hand Tracker with Remote recording on (a camera rig on a Raspberry Pi, say): pick its cameras and mode, fill in the take details and watch each camera live.

    Over Tailscale (from anywhere, with Tailscale on this device): its Tailscale name, such as pi or pi.your-tailnet.ts.net, or its 100.x address. No code is needed: only your own devices can reach it there.

    On the same Wi-Fi without Tailscale: paste the address under the QR code in Hand Tracker's Remote recording (it ends in #k= and a code), or scan the QR code with a phone's camera. The code is what that page needs.
